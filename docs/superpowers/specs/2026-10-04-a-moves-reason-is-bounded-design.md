# A move's reason is bounded, and its tool says who reads it

## The problem

`TokenMoved.reason` (`contract/vtt/v1/events.proto`) is free text a move's
issuer writes, and nothing bounds its length but `maxWSFrameBytes` in
`internal/gateway/server.go`, the 32768-byte read limit on a WebSocket frame.
`engine.Apply`'s `TokenMoved` arm (`internal/engine/apply.go`) checks the
token and the destination and never reads the reason, and `client/src/fold.ts`'s
`tokenMoved` arm does the same. The fold bounds five other texts: a note's
key 128 bytes (`maxNoteKeyBytes`), a note's title 256 (`maxNoteTitleBytes`), a
narration's speaker 256 (`maxNarrationAsBytes`), and narration and note text
8192 (`maxTextBytes`), each refused with an error the command answers; no
other text has a bound in the fold, so one a command carries is bounded only
by `maxWSFrameBytes`, and one a map or adventure file carries, a scene's name
among them, by nothing. `fold.ts` mirrors four of the five
with `checkLen`, which counts UTF-8 bytes as Go does; its `narrationAdded` arm
never checks the speaker, so a DM client folds an `as` that `engine.Apply`
refuses. A reason is shown on one line of the feed and the
ticker, after the move's label (`describe` in `client/src/view/spectator.ts`),
and the log keeps it forever. The MCP `move_token` tool's schema
(`contract/gen/tools/tools.json`, generated by `tools/toolgen` from its
`manifest`) gives `reason` a type and no description, so an agent offered the
field is not told that only the DM and the agent read it (SPEC-016) or how long
it may be; `toolgen`'s `fieldOverride.fieldDocs` is how a tool's field gets a
description, and `add_narration` uses it.

## Done looks like

1. A `move_token` whose reason is longer than 256 bytes is refused and appends
   nothing, and one of exactly 256 bytes is accepted: named tests under
   `internal/engine/` and `internal/gateway/` fail on today's tree and pass
   after; the refusal is the fold's, so a `TokenMoved` with such a reason
   reaching `engine.Apply` by any path is refused.
2. `client/src/fold.ts` refuses the same `TokenMoved` as `engine.Apply`, at
   the same byte count: a named test under `client/test/` fails on today's
   tree and passes after.
3. The MCP `move_token` tool's `reason` has a description saying that it is
   optional, that only the DM and the agent read it, and its bound;
   `contract/gen/tools/tools.json` and `cmd/vtt/tools.json` are regenerated,
   `contract/testdata/expected_tools.json` agrees, and `task check:drift` is
   clean.
4. Every golden stream under `scenarios/goldens/` is byte-identical, since no
   scenario gives a move a reason.
5. `task check` whole is green.
6. `client/src/fold.ts` refuses a `NarrationAdded` whose `as` is longer than
   256 bytes, as `engine.Apply` does: a named test under `client/test/` fails
   on today's tree and passes after (the owner's ruling at sign-off).

## Rules this puts on the system

- A move's reason is at most 256 bytes.
- A move whose reason is longer is refused and appends nothing.
- The tool that offers a move's reason says who reads it and how long it may
  be.
- The client's fold refuses a narration speaker the server's fold refuses.

## What it touches

1. `internal/engine/apply.go` (`Apply`'s `TokenMoved` arm and its constants)
   and its tests
2. `client/src/fold.ts` (`tokenMoved`) and its tests under `client/test/`;
   `cmd/vtt/webdist` (rebuilt)
3. `tools/toolgen/main.go` (`manifest`'s `move_token`), the regenerated
   `contract/gen/tools/tools.json` and `cmd/vtt/tools.json`, and
   `contract/testdata/expected_tools.json`
4. `internal/gateway` tests, for the refusal over the wire
5. `docs/specifications/013-authorization.md`, which states that a move's
   reason is carried; a new specification of the fold's byte bounds;
   `docs/requirements.md`
6. `tools/toolgen/main_test.go`, a new internal test in `internal/engine`,
   `tools/mutation-equivalents.txt` and `tools/ts-mutation-equivalents.txt`
   (keys moved by the new lines), `tools/comment-ceilings.txt`

Three components, the fold first, then the client's mirror, then the tool;
none of them can be split from the bound it states without one layer
accepting what another refuses.

## Specifications this moves

docs/specifications/013-authorization.md
New: the fold's byte bounds on an event's free text, every bounded field, its
bound, and the client's mirror.

## What could not be established

- Whether 256 is the bound. It is the owner's proposal of 2026-10-04, matching
  the fold's other short labels (`maxNoteTitleBytes`, `maxNarrationAsBytes`);
  the plan confirms or argues against it at sign-off.
- Whether an empty reason stays allowed. Today it means no reason (VTT-261),
  unlike a narration's text, whose bound starts at one byte; the plan says.
- Whether the bound and its refusal belong in SPEC-013, which states that a
  move's reason is carried, or in a record of the fold, which has none; the
  plan decides and names the file.
- Whether the tool's description should state the number, which then has to
  move with the constant, or only that the reason is short.
